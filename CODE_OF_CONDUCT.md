# Code of Conduct

Voxelia is committed to a professional, respectful and technically rigorous
community.

## Expected behaviour

Participants shall:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on ideas, evidence and implementation;
- welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- protect confidential and patient information;
- give credit for work and sources; and
- accept maintainers' decisions made through documented governance.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, release of
confidential information, plagiarism and knowingly misleading validation or
benchmark evidence are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Community-conduct concerns may be reported privately to the project governance
contacts listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Security vulnerabilities shall use the
process in `SECURITY.md`.

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or take other
proportionate action after reviewing a report.
